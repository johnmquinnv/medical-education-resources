# Public-health research methods

**Maintainer:** John Quinn, MD, MPH, PhD, EMT-P  
**Version:** 0.1.0 · **Updated:** 22 October 2026  
**Status:** original portfolio demonstration; not a validated instrument or approved operational policy. Synthetic examples only.

Practical starting templates for surveys, structured interviews, focus groups, Delphi exercises and mixed-methods analysis. These demonstrate a workflow from a defined question to documented findings; they are not reports of a completed study.

The worked example asks which course-design features adult learners find useful. All sample responses are invented. No participant recruitment or data collection has occurred for this demonstration.

## Use and governance

Document the purpose, approvals, participant information, consent process, lawful data handling and retention before collecting real responses. Obtain a local determination of whether the activity is research, evaluation or quality improvement. A public GitHub issue is not a channel for participant responses. Store real data in an approved private system.

Google Forms and Excel are example tools, not endorsements of their suitability for confidential data in any particular organisation. Account configuration and organisational approval matter.

## Reporting resources

- [COREQ](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/coreq/): reporting interviews and focus groups.
- [DELPHISTAR](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/delphi-studies-in-social-and-health-sciences-recommendations-for-an-interdisciplinary-standardized-reporting-delphistar-results-of-a-delphi-study/): reporting Delphi studies in health and social sciences.
- [CREDES](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/credes/): Delphi guidance developed in palliative care; consider its scope when applying it elsewhere.
- [Braun and Clarke: Reflecting on reflexive thematic analysis](https://uwe-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/1493242): methodological differences between thematic-analysis approaches.

These resources support method selection and transparent reporting; linking them does not validate these templates.
