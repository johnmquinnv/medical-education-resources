# Delphi, modified Delphi and qualitative coding

**Maintainer:** John Quinn, MD, MPH, PhD, EMT-P  
**Version:** 0.1.0 · **Updated:** 22 October 2026  
**Status:** original portfolio demonstration; not a validated instrument or approved operational policy. Synthetic examples only.

## Delphi planning worksheet

- Define the decision and why a consensus method is suitable.
- Define expertise, recruitment, stakeholder representation and conflicts of interest.
- Choose a conventional or modified design; describe any initial item list, evidence review or workshop.
- Specify the rating scale, consensus and disagreement rules before responses are seen.
- Specify anonymity arrangements, feedback, number of rounds and stopping rules.
- Pilot items for clarity and assess accessibility and language needs.

### Illustrative process

Round 1 gathers ratings and optional reasons. Summarise the full distribution and dissent, then return controlled, non-identifying feedback. Round 2 permits rerating after feedback. Apply the predeclared rule and report attrition and unresolved items. A live workshop changes the anonymity and group-influence conditions; disclose this for a modified Delphi.

No universal percentage threshold is prescribed here. Consensus is an agreement measure, not proof of clinical efficacy or safety.

## Qualitative codebook example

| Code | Definition | Include | Exclude | Invented example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Practice opportunity | Learner describes chance to rehearse | Repetition, time to practise | General enjoyment | “A second attempt helped me organise the handover.” |
| Language access | Language affects participation | Translation, vocabulary | Equipment issues | “The bilingual glossary helped with unfamiliar terms.” |
| Feedback specificity | Feedback identifies an actionable behaviour | Concrete observations | Generic praise | “The observer showed me which detail I omitted.” |

Choose and explain the analytic approach. For a structured codebook approach, pilot coding, document revisions and discuss divergent interpretations. For reflexive thematic analysis, explain researcher interpretation and reflexivity; do not automatically impose inter-rater agreement metrics as a universal quality requirement.

Keep a decision trail from excerpt to code to theme. Search for contradictory examples. Do not imply that the frequency of a code measures population prevalence. Integrate qualitative and quantitative results in a table that makes agreements, differences and missing evidence visible.
