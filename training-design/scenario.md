# Fictional coordination exercise

**Maintainer:** John Quinn, MD, MPH, PhD, EMT-P  
**Version:** 0.1.0 · **Updated:** 22 October 2026  
**Status:** original portfolio demonstration; not a validated instrument or approved operational policy. Synthetic examples only.

## Facilitator brief

A fictional adult-learning course is moving a simulated casualty between two training stations. The exercise concerns coordination and documentation only. No clinical treatment or transport decision is assessed.

## Starting information

The sending coordinator has confirmed a receiving station but has not recorded who accepted the handover. A document uses an unexplained abbreviation. Two participants believe the other person is responsible for the next update.

## Injects

1. At five minutes, the receiving station asks for clarification of the abbreviation.
2. At eight minutes, the planned communications channel becomes unavailable within the simulation.
3. At eleven minutes, an observer asks who owns the unresolved action and when it will be reviewed.

## Expected observable behaviours

- Identify the missing acceptance and responsibility information.
- Clarify language without inventing facts.
- Confirm a communication alternative defined by the exercise facilitator.
- Assign an owner and review point for unresolved actions.
- Confirm mutual understanding before closing the handover.

Pause if learners move into real clinical advice, reveal identifiable cases, or experience distress. Debrief process and teamwork, not personal blame.
