# Pilot protocol: coordination and handover exercise

**Owner:** John Quinn · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Prepared:** 31 October 2026  
**Status:** ready for faculty review; no participant pilot or external sign-off recorded.

## Purpose and decision

Test whether the fictional [scenario](scenario.md), facilitator instructions and [observation worksheet](assessment.md) are understandable and usable. The decision is which instructions or activities to revise before a wider educational session.

## Preparation

Assign a facilitator, an observer and a faculty reviewer. Record intended learner roles, language needs, setting and the local determination of whether the exercise is teaching, evaluation or research. Explain participation, recording choices, feedback use and storage. Use fictional information throughout. Agree a stop signal and a way to pause without disadvantage.

## Conduct

Use the 60-minute structure in the [curriculum map](README.md). Before the exercise, ask learners to restate their roles and the handover objective. Deliver each scenario inject at the specified time. The observer records behavior and context using the rubric, distinguishing not observed from not applicable. Debrief what was intended, what happened and one feasible improvement; repeat the affected segment if appropriate.

## Evidence to record

| Question | Evidence | Revision trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Are instructions understood? | Questions asked and learner restatement | Ambiguous roles, unexplained language or conflicting instructions |
| Can the observer use the rubric? | Missing or disputed observations and context | Criteria that cannot be observed or consistently interpreted |
| Is the timing workable? | Actual phase timings and omitted activities | Important activities rushed or missed |
| Are materials accessible? | Language, readability and participation feedback | Any learner unable to participate meaningfully |
| Does the repeat show a process change? | Specific observed behavior before and after | Unresolved coordination failure or an unintended consequence |

Avoid universal pass thresholds for this unvalidated rubric. Describe observations and denominators; do not present participant enjoyment or improved rehearsal as evidence of clinical benefit.

## Record and close

Record date, setting, learner roles, invited/participating counts, deviations, observations, feedback and changes. Keep identities and individual responses private. Enter a public summary and review decision in the [portfolio review record](https://github.com/johnmquinnv/johnmquinnv/blob/main/REVIEW-RECORD.md) only when authorized. Report unresolved issues and the next pilot version.
