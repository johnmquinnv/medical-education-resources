## Debrief prompts

1. What was intended?
2. What happened, based on observation?
3. What conditions helped or hindered the team?
4. What will we retain or change?
5. What evidence would show improvement on a repeat attempt?

## Evaluation plan

Separate participant reaction, demonstrated learning, workplace behaviour and outcomes. A positive feedback score cannot establish changes in patient outcomes. Define follow-up measures and feasible attribution before making effectiveness claims.
